Charles Brophy

Historian of Malaya and Singapore

ORCIDX (Twitter)Email

I work on the political history of Southeast Asia, with a focus on Malaya and Singapore. My research sits at the intersection of historical sociology, Marxist political theory, and postcolonial studies, and is concerned with how colonial capitalism, communalism, and uneven development shaped the politics of decolonisation and the postcolonial state.
